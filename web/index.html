🛠️ Critical Touch & Twist Removal Protocol (Do This While Warm)

STEP 1

Shut Down Immediately

Click the Full Shutdown button below. Power off immediately so the heat doesn't dissipate before you remove the cooler.

STEP 2

Disconnect Power

Flip the power supply rocker switch to OFF (O) and disconnect the AC wall power cable.

STEP 3

Loosen Retention Screws

Loosen the cooler mounting thumbscrews or unlatch the AMD retention clips evenly in small diagonal turns.

STEP 4

GENTLE TWIST FIRST!

NEVER PULL STRAIGHT UP! Grasp cooler base and gently rotate it 5° clockwise and counter-clockwise to shear the fluid paste. Once it moves freely, gently lift it off.
